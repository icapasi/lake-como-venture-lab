CleanOS

thecleanos.com ↗

Preventing oral disease with devices made to fit.

The problem

Oral diseases are among the most common in the world and largely preventable. Yet daily hygiene still relies on standard toothbrushes and each person's technique.

The solution

Smart Brush: one handle and two brush heads, one for the upper arch and one for the lower, shaped on each person's mouth from a dental scan, an at-home impression kit or the dentist's impression. It cleans the whole arch in seconds.

Team
Giovanni Tuccari (CEO), Pietro Pastore (CMO, dentist), Francesco Anzalone (CTO, robotics engineer)
  • Founded in 2024
  • Based in Milan

MedTech

Fraido

fraido.it ↗

An intubation tube that adapts to the patient.

The problem

Difficult intubations often need several devices in sequence, first a guide and then the tube. Each step adds time, and in an emergency seconds count.

The solution

An endotracheal tube with an adjustable diameter. It goes in thin, like a guide, and once in place it is mechanically expanded to the size that fits the patient's trachea: one device instead of two.

Team
Antonio Maria Vizioli (emergency nurse), Elia Fregolese (materials engineer)
  • Patented device
  • Forbes Under 30 · 2026
  • Advisors from 14 hospitals in Italy and the UK
P
Mechatronics

Prometheus Plasma Systems

prometheusplasmasystems.com ↗

Plasma technology, from industry to nuclear fusion.

The problem

Industrial vacuum pumps rely on rotating parts. Bearings wear out, vibration and magnetic fields limit where they can be used, and every stoppage has a cost.

The solution

A patented architecture with coaxial electrodes and electromagnetic flow control that moves gas with no moving parts. It leads to three product lines: plasma diagnostics and power systems, solid-state vacuum pumps and, in the long run, a fusion reactor.

Team
Founders with experience at Thales Alenia Space and a PhD in plasma physics from Max Planck IPP
  • Patent granted · PCT pending
  • Plasma diagnostics: TRL 8
  • Gamma-5 vacuum pump: TRL 4
