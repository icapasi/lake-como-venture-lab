A strategic asset

Research and industry on the same campus

For a hardware startup, the most delicate step is the one between the idea and the first Proof of Concept: it takes expensive equipment, repeatable tests and people who know how to interpret the results. The programme makes exactly these resources available, on the Lecco campus.

The laboratories cover robotics, sensing, metrology, motion analysis and advanced manufacturing: fields directly aligned with the programme's two verticals. And, based on the needs of each project, expertise and infrastructure from the entire Politecnico di Milano laboratory network can also be activated.

The laboratories

Six laboratories, complementary expertise

We-Cobot

Collaborative robotics
MechatronicsMedTech

Develops robots designed to work alongside people. Projects include Tiago, an elderly care robot being trialled in Italian and Swiss facilities, and research on exoskeletons for rehabilitation and for supporting people with disabilities: the frontier of wearable robotics.

Luca Pozzi, Nicole Sanna, researchers

IndexLab

Creative robotics and digital fabrication
Mechatronics

Uses robotics and 3D printing to create objects and structural components that could not be produced with traditional methods, including in construction. It already works with companies that turn to the Politecnico to transform their ideas into products.

Pierpaolo Ruttico, researcher

MetroSpace

Metrology for extreme environments · Spacetech
Mechatronics

Designs instruments that measure temperature, time, vibration and force, able to work precisely even in space and other complex environments, where data reliability is critical.

Prof. Diego Scaccabarozzi, scientific lead

Human Performance Lab

Human motion analysis
MedTech

Studies movement with cutting-edge instrumentation, including an immersive simulator that reproduces different sports conditions. It supports performance improvement and, above all, injury prevention.

Pietro Maver, lab technician

SensibiLab

Wearable biomedical sensors and systems
MedTech

Combines textiles, electronics and sensors in wearable devices that monitor posture and movement, make diagnosis more accurate and support rehabilitation and assistive technologies for people with disabilities.

Prof. Giuseppe Andreoni, scientific lead

Bioinspired System

Soft robotics and underwater propulsion
Mechatronics

Develops nature-inspired robots with soft, flexible surfaces that move through water more efficiently. A large indoor tank makes it possible to test aquatic drones and robot fish.

Giovanni Bianchi, researcher

The Lecco advantage

Laboratories that work together

The distinctive feature of the Lecco campus is proximity: the laboratories are arranged across the campus to encourage exchange and collaboration. A startup can therefore combine several areas of expertise on the same project: when expertise adds up, the result grows more than proportionally.

One project, several laboratories

For example, a rehabilitation device can bring together wearable sensors, collaborative robotics and motion analysis.

Researchers as partners

Direct access to qualified researchers and technicians, with scientific mentoring throughout the programme.
